The four Denavit-Hartenberg parameters are:

1. The joint angle (θ)
2. The link length (a)
3. The link twist (d)
4. The joint offset (h)

These parameters are used in the Denavit-Hartenberg convention to describe the kinematic structure of a robotic manipulator.

[biblio 69-3-2-forward-kinematics-the-denavit-hartenberg-convention.md:17-26, 471 tok, 11s]
The four Denavit-Hartenberg parameters are _θi_ (joint angle), _ai_ (link length), _di_ (link offset), and _αi_ (link twist).
